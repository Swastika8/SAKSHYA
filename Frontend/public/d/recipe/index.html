An easy vegetable khichdi for slow evenings, busy weekdays and whatever is in your fridge.

Prep 10 minCook 25 minServes 3
Simple & seasonal

Vegetable khichdi

Rice, lentils and colourful vegetables simmered together. Finish with a little ghee and serve warm.

Difficulty: easy
Equipment: one saucepan with a lid

What you need

  • ½ cup rice
  • ½ cup split yellow moong dal
  • 1 cup diced carrot, peas and beans
  • 1 tablespoon ghee or oil
  • ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon grated ginger
  • 3½ cups water, more if needed
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander and lemon to finish

Make it at home

  1. Rinse and soak. Rinse the rice and dal until the water is mostly clear. Soak for 10 minutes, then drain.
  2. Start the aromatics. Warm the ghee in a saucepan. Add cumin and ginger. Stir for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the vegetables. Stir in the diced vegetables and turmeric. Cook for 2 minutes.
  4. Simmer gently. Add the rice, dal, water and salt. Bring to a boil, cover and reduce the heat. Simmer for about 25 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Check the texture. The rice and dal should be tender. Add hot water for a softer consistency, and cook a little longer if needed.
  6. Finish and serve. Rest for 5 minutes. Add coriander, lemon and an optional spoonful of ghee.

Little kitchen notes

For a softer bowl, add an extra half cup of water. Swap the vegetables for what you have. Cool leftovers promptly, refrigerate and reheat thoroughly with a splash of water.

# Verification required before the demo

Every provision deliberately has `needs_verification: true`. These are brief paraphrases, not statutory quotations or legal advice. Verify **every section number, wording, amendment, commencement date, exception and applicability** against [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/) and a qualified Indian lawyer before a public demo or use in a real case. Sources were consulted on 5 October 2026; that does not certify legal correctness.

The corpus has 18 provisions. BNS is relevant only subject to commencement and incident dates; older incidents may require different law. Online impersonation does not automatically establish IT Act 66C/66D; a synthetic image does not automatically meet every voyeurism or privacy element. Provisions identify issues for review, not guilt. Section 66A is intentionally excluded.

BSA section 63 and its Schedule have party and expert portions. The generated preparation worksheet is a paraphrased draft, **not the official Schedule or a completed court certificate**. Have an expert/lawyer complete and verify the prescribed form, device facts and attached hash report. Hashing cannot prove authenticity, authorship, an independent timestamp, or admissibility.

`routes.json` is a separate, allowlisted corpus of reporting resources. Check links before demonstrations. StopNCII is for eligible adults and content taken at 18 or older; use the age-appropriate Take It Down instructions for images taken under 18. Never obtain or redistribute illegal material merely to make a hash. No external report is submitted by this prototype.

The multilingual retriever uses in-memory normalized embeddings and numpy cosine similarity when its model is available, otherwise scikit-learn character TF-IDF. A keyword eligibility gate prevents unsupported topics from retrieving arbitrary laws. This small corpus is not comprehensive; missing results must not be interpreted as absence of a legal remedy.
